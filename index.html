Seven years of honoring the women behind the work

Celebrating power • legacy • strength

This is HER night.

Since the beginning, the beHER Awards has sought to honor women who are doing the work but are not always being seen. The women serving behind the scenes.

The women building businesses, strengthening families, leading ministries, mentoring the next generation, transforming communities, and creating opportunities for others. The women whose names may never make the headlines, but whose impact can be felt everywhere they go.

This night was created for HER. For the woman who kept showing up when no one was applauding. For the woman who gave, served, sacrificed, built, prayed, encouraged, and led, not for recognition, but because purpose required it. For the woman whose work deserves to be seen, whose story deserves to be heard, and whose legacy deserves to be celebrated.

At the beHER Awards, we believe recognition should not be reserved only for those with the largest platforms or the most recognizable names. Sometimes the most extraordinary women are quietly changing the world one life, one family, one business, one ministry, and one community at a time.

And on this night, we see HER. We honor her strength. We celebrate her power. We recognize her impact.

We tell her story. We give her the flowers she deserves.

Because she has spent enough time celebrating everyone else.
This is her moment. This is her celebration. This is HER Night.

The woman behind the vision

Dr. Carolyn
Horne Robins

Founder & visionary | beHER

As the visionary behind the beHER Awards, Dr. Carolyn Horne Robins has dedicated over seven years to building a sanctuary for Black excellence. Her leadership is defined by a commitment to power, legacy, and strength, ensuring that extraordinary women have a platform where their stories are not only heard but celebrated with the prestige they deserve. Under her guidance, the awards have evolved into a national beacon of inspiration and cultural impact.

“We are the architects of our own legacy, building a future where our power is undeniable.”
